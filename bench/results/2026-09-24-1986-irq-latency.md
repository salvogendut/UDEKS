# Initial `1986` interrupt-entry results

Status: **preliminary, emulator-only, not an executive-CPU decision**.

Both candidate interrupt routes completed 32 consecutive CIA1 Timer-A
interrupts in each of three runs. Every run reported CIA1 ICR `$81` on its
first and last entry, no unexpected sources, and the complete target count.

| Configuration | Minimum | Median | Maximum | Spread |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8502, 1 MHz | 25 | 53.5 | 82 | 57 |
| 8502, 2 MHz | 13 | 41.5 | 74 | 61 |
| Z80, stock timing | 57 | 87 | 117 | 60 |

All values are one-megahertz system ticks; lower is better. Each row summarizes
32 samples, and all three runs of a configuration produced the same sample
sequence. The adjacent JSON file preserves those sequences.

## What is measured

CIA1 Timer A runs continuously with a 1,000-tick period. A sample starts when
the timer underflows and ends at the first counter read after the interrupt
handler has preserved the registers admitted by its proposed ISR ABI:

- The 8502 hardware stacks P and PC; the prologue additionally stacks A, X,
  and Y before reading the timer.
- The Z80 enters IM1 with PC stacked and interrupts disabled; the prologue
  additionally stacks AF, BC, DE, HL, IX, and IY. It does not admit alternate
  registers or I/R, because the handler does not use them.

Consequently, this is an executive-relevant entry-plus-prologue measurement,
not the bare processor interrupt-acceptance time and not a full task-context
switch. The different register contracts are intentional and are part of the
cost being evaluated.

## Interpretation

The probe establishes two useful facts: native 8502 vectors work in the planned
bank-zero mapping, and Z80 IM1 works with bottom and top 16 KiB common RAM after
the reset BIOS overlay is removed. Neither candidate is blocked from owning
CIA interrupts.

The latency figures themselves are not yet suitable for choosing the executive.
All three configurations show a deterministic stair-step sequence with a
roughly 60-tick spread. That common shape is much larger than instruction-level
acceptance jitter and indicates `1986`'s interrupt scheduling granularity. Real
hardware and VICE must decide whether any physical difference remains. The
subsequent [service-cost suite](2026-09-24-1986-irq-service.md) times
acknowledgement, representative work, and return separately.
