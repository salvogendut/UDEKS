# Dedication

The name **UDEKS** echoes the Latin word *iudex*: “judge.”

This project is dedicated to **Lo Giudice**, a teacher who taught me
programming when I was a child.

He fully embodied authority, knowledge, and insight. His direction was firm,
yet it was always joined with paternal sweetness. What he taught reached far
beyond the mechanics of programming: it showed how knowledge can be carried
with clarity, purpose, patience, and care.

UDEKS is developed in his memory.
